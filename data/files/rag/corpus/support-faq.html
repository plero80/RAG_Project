Support FAQ

Support hours are 08:00 to 16:00, Monday to Thursday.

When can I reach support?

Support hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, Central European Time.

How do I book a repair?

Email support@quillmere.example with the serial number of the unit. Repairs are usually completed within 10 working days of the unit arriving.

Is my data kept during a repair?

Stored readings are kept unless the memory board is replaced, in which case we tell you first.
